Crop Physiology

☀️ Photosynthesis & Transpiration

  • C3 vs. C4 vs. CAM — C4 crops (corn, sugarcane) utilize PEP carboxylase to avoid photorespiration under high light/temp.
  • Stomatal Conductance — guard cells regulate CO₂ influx and transpirational cooling based on light, humidity, and ABA.
  • Source-Sink Dynamics — photoassimilates translocate from mature leaves (sources) to filling grains or tubers (sinks).

Agronomic Crop Production

🌾 Grain & Industrial Crops

  • Plant Density — population per hectare = 10,000 m² ÷ (row spacing × hill spacing) × plants/hill.
  • Rice Ecosystems — irrigated lowland, rainfed lowland, and upland environments determine cultivar and water regime.
  • Cropping Systems — multiple cropping, intercropping, and crop rotation optimize land-use efficiency and suppress pests.

🥦 High-Value Horticultural Crops

Plant Breeding & Genetics

🧬 Cultivar Development & Selection

  • Mating Systems — self-pollinated crops (pureline selection, pedigree) vs. cross-pollinated crops (heterosis, synthetics).
  • Heterosis (Hybrid Vigor) — superiority of F1 hybrid progeny over the mean or better of their parental lines.
  • Biotechnology Tools — marker-assisted selection (MAS) and genetic transformation accelerate cultivar development.

Plant Propagation & Seed Tech

🌱 Seed Testing & Asexual Propagation

  • Germination Percentage — (normal seedlings ÷ total seeds planted) × 100; evaluates seed lot viability.
  • Seed Classes in PH — Breeder Seed (white tag) → Foundation Seed (red) → Registered Seed (green) → Certified Seed (blue).
  • Grafting & Budding — unites scion and rootstock via cambial alignment for clonal multiplication of fruit crops.

Post-Harvest Handling & Technology

📦 Maintaining Quality & Reducing Losses

  • Climacteric vs. Non-Climacteric — climacteric fruits (banana, mango) exhibit an ethylene and respiration burst during ripening.
  • Temperature & Cold Chain — precooling and low storage temperature slow respiration, moisture loss, and pathogen decay.
  • Harvest Index (HI) — ratio of economic yield (e.g., grain) to total biological aboveground biomass.
